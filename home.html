    Bring in your calendars

    Import a calendar file and its next two weeks show up in Coming up, next to your scholarship and program deadlines.

    • Canvas: Calendar → Calendar Feed → open the link, and it downloads a .ics file.
    • Google Calendar: Settings → Import & export → Export, then unzip.
    • Outlook / Apple Calendar: export or share the calendar as .ics.

      Imported calendars are saved on this device.
