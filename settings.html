About you

Appearance

Theme

Home becomes the first comp. menu, with 3D spider lilies that bloom as you hover. It stays light in dark mode.

Use it like an app

Install comp. on your phone or computer to open it from your home screen. It works offline too.

On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

Your data on this device

Without an account, your hours, lists, calendars and settings live only in this browser. Clearing your browser data erases them, so download a backup now and then.

A backup is a .json file. Restoring replaces what's on this device with the backup.
